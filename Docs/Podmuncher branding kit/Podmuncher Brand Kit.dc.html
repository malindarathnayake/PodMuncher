01

Positioning

For people who would rather read, search, or quote audio than scrub through it, Podmuncher is an open-source command-line tool that turns podcasts and other recordings into transcripts and subtitles on your own machine. It runs locally and works offline once set up, so your audio stays with you.

Tagline options
A
Audio in. Text out.
Recommended primary. Plain, says exactly what happens.
B
Feed it audio. Read the leftovers.
The playful one. Good for the mascot, stickers, social.
C
Your recordings, readable. On your machine.
Leads with privacy and local processing for research and newsroom audiences.
02

Three directions

Each was sketched far enough to judge the symbol at 16px, a terminal, and a README. 1a is developed into the full identity below.

1a · Recommended podmuncher
Chomp
Bricolage Grotesque · Atkinson Hyperlegible · JetBrains Mono

An arcade-style wedge mouth caught mid-bite on two waveform bars. The eye and single fang give it a personality; warm orange on cream keeps it friendly without a gradient in sight. Reads instantly at 16px and animates naturally (open/closed) for progress states.

1b
podmuncher_
Feeder
IBM Plex Mono · IBM Plex Sans

A machine slot: waveform goes in, lines come out. Terminal-native and very credible with developers. The creature is implied, not shown, so it loses the mischief the name promises and sits close to many other CLI identities.

1c
p dmuncher
Bite
Newsreader · IBM Plex Sans

Editorial and reading-first: a bite taken out of the “o”, a highlighter stroke across the text. Speaks to journalists and researchers. The bitten “o” is weak as a standalone icon and the serif feels distant in a terminal.

03

Logo

The symbol is a muncher mid-bite on two waveform bars. The wordmark is set lowercase in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold to match the podmuncher command. In running brand copy, write “Podmuncher”.

Construction & clear space
¼ h ¼ h
  • Body: circle r = 22 on a 64 grid, mouth 70° opening, centered on the horizontal axis.
  • Waveform bars: 5 units wide, 2.5 radius, heights 20 and 11.
  • Clear space on all sides: ¼ of the symbol height (h). For lockups, use ¼ of the symbol height too.
Minimum size
16 px
24 px
podmuncher
lockup · 100 px wide
  • Symbol: 16 px digital, 6 mm print. Below 24 px, the favicon cut (no fang) is used.
  • Horizontal lockup: 100 px wide digital, 30 mm print.
  • Primary stacked logo: 72 px tall digital. Below that, switch to horizontal.

08

Voice & tone

Useful first
Every message says what happened and what to do next. The joke, if any, comes after the information.
One munch per message
Food words (munch, chew, leftovers) at most once per message, and never in an error’s first line.
First person, short
Podmuncher speaks as “I”. Sentences under ~15 words. No exclamation marks in errors.
Technical when it helps
Name Docker, models, paths and flags exactly when the user needs them; keep them out of headlines.
Say · “Munched episode.mp3.” · “I couldn’t find that file.” · “One-time download.” · “Runs on your machine.”
Avoid · “Oops!! Something went wrong 😅” · “Blazing-fast AI magic” · “Nom nom nom” · unverified speed or accuracy claims

Assets, licenses & notes

Files in this kit
assets/README.md — full inventory
assets/svg/ — editable vector logos, mascot, icons, banner, social card
assets/png/ — transparent PNG exports (banner and social card are opaque by design)

Lockup SVGs keep the wordmark as live text set in Bricolage Grotesque so it stays editable. Convert text to outlines before distributing to people who may not have the font. PNG lockups were rendered with the font embedded.

Licenses

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Atkinson Hyperlegible, JetBrains Mono (and IBM Plex Mono, Newsreader in the explorations) are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, available from Google Fonts.

Artwork: all marks and the mascot are original geometric drawings made for this kit; no external images are used. Suggested: release artwork under CC BY 4.0 and state in the README that the name and logo identify the official project (a separate trademark policy, not covered by Apache-2.0).

Mockup notes

Terminal output, file names (episode.timed.txt), setup steps, the domain and the landing page are proposals. No download counts, testimonials, integrations or performance claims are shown.

The wedge-mouth riff is deliberately differentiated from classic arcade characters: orange instead of yellow, a fang, an eye, waveform bars instead of dots, and feet on the mascot. Run a trademark search before registering.
